# Project Brief — Single design and task handoff record

This is the **master template**, not an active game. Complete it collaboratively when creating a game. This file **records** decisions and approvals; it never grants them. Only the user can approve scope, in their message in the current conversation. **Undecided = ask, don't choose.**

## Game decisions (user-confirmed only)

- **Game title:** HyperCars (user)
- **Godot version (pin minor, e.g. 4.x):** 4.7.x — developed and tested with 4.7.2-stable, GL Compatibility renderer (user, 2026-11-14)
- **Premise / core player loop:** **Option B, approved 2026-11-22 (user, "The Road to 1.0"):** a complete arcade combat racer with a finite career and an ending, playable indefinitely afterwards. Race → compete against familiar rivals → earn money → maintain and equip the car → win championships → advance through the career → become the HyperCars Champion. Reference `docs/CAREER.md`.
- **Perspective / art direction:** **Approved 2026-11-14 (user, "HyperCars — Approved Visual Direction").** Top-down view. Original, modern, hand-painted HyperCars identity; do **not** reproduce the original Super Cars II pixel art.
  - **Style reference:** the user's own *Fluffy Bears of Doom* drawings in `docs/art_refrence/` (folder name as committed): `bomb`, `chair`, `clown`, `duck`, `effects`, `fluff`, `powerups`, `tiles` (`.png`).
  - **Study only their artistic characteristics:** hand-painted textures, soft brushwork and organic outlines; slightly imperfect, asymmetrical shapes with personality; soft shading, highlights and subtle surface texture; their colour choices, proportions and playful character. **Never recreate or incorporate the depicted objects** (bomb, furniture, bear, chest, clown, duck, etc.).
  - **Applies to** original HyperCars artwork: top-down cars, tracks, terrain, obstacles, weapons, interface elements and other assets as required.
  - **Asset rules:** `template_` graphics must be proper, visually appealing art, not programmer placeholders; all assets stay stylistically consistent so the user's drawings can replace them one at a time; user artwork has priority and missing custom assets fall back to Claude's templates through the existing `custom/` override contract (`assets/README.md`), including replacement in exported Windows/Linux games without rebuilding; consistent dimensions, transparency, alignment and animation/sprite-sheet layouts must be defined; create only assets needed for approved iterations.
- **Confirmed mechanics and constraints (current rules, 2026-11-17; each stated once here; details in the linked docs):**
  - **Fidelity.** Gameplay stays mechanically faithful to Super Cars II (1991, Amiga); art follows the approved visual direction. Numbers are provisional until calibrated against the original. **M1 handling is the approved baseline**: any change to driving physics needs user approval.
  - **Controls** (manual, 2026-11-14): steer left / right; *Fire to Accelerate* (Fire held accelerates, release brakes) or *Fire to Brake* (automatic acceleration, Fire brakes hard); forward / backwards fire the front / rear weapon and never brake.
    - **Remapping:** every player action can be remapped on CONTROLS, with two keyboard and two controller inputs each (`src/controls/control_bindings.gd`, saved to `user://controls.cfg`). Clashes are refused and RESTORE DEFAULTS resets.
    - **Devices:** single-player controller bindings work on any connected pad; couch players get copies bound to their own pad. Unplugging a racing player's pad pauses a local race.
    - **Menus:** Enter / keypad Enter / pad Start (Confirm) continue and Esc / pad B (Back) go back. Enter and Esc are fixed and cannot be remapped away.
    - **Fire never dismisses** results, standings, rewards, forfeits or the garage. Fire starts the race on the grid panel and readies in the network lobby.
    - **Hints:** on-screen hints show the player's current bindings.
  - **Race rules:** the finishing order reverses the next race's grid (winner starts last). Race lengths are 3 / 7 / 20 / 50 laps (default 3, saved); Knockout and championship rounds have fixed lengths. **Weapons Hot:** weapons are locked for everyone until the whole field has crossed the start line (15 s safety), never in No Guns. Rear-quarter contact yaw is the same for all. Reference `docs/RACE_RULES.md`.
  - **Rivals:** racer ids 1–9 are fixed identities: Victor "The Professor", Rocco "Mad Dog", Ellie "Sparrow", Hank "The Wall", Maya "Ghost", Dante "Showboat", Greta "Clockwork", Finn "Wildcard", Nina "The Hunter" (`src/ai/rival_roster.gd`).
    - Personalities live in `src/ai/personalities/` (user-approved descriptions; Victor's tuning is the user's commit 8d58ccd).
    - Rivals drive with player-equivalent inputs, personal racing lines and race-length ammo pacing. They avoid wrecks imperfectly (pass through a wreck after 0.5 s held up, never an active car), and Micro Nemesis affects targeting only.
    - **Race variety:** each new race scene gets its own AI seed from a session seed (`--seed=N` reproduces a session; race scenes built directly keep seed 1991 for tests).
  - **Combat** (verified from the manual): one FRONT and one REAR weapon type per race.
    - Front / rear missiles fly straight; homing missiles home on the nearest car; the super missile orbits its car; mines explode after a few seconds.
    - A destroyed car is wrecked for a while, then continues.
    - Weapons ON / No Guns is a setup option. Slots (HyperCars decision): front = front / homing / super missiles, rear = rear missiles / mines.
    - **Combat styles:** *Classic* (shared 0.6 s cooldown) and *Tactical* (front 1.5 s, rear 3 s, 0.35 s shared lock), configurable in `WeaponRules` (`src/combat/default_weapon_rules.tres`).
    - **Ammunition** scales with race length (×1 / 2 / 5 / 10 for 3 / 7 / 20 / 50 laps), except the garage player's own rounds.
    - **Weapon cycling** (2026-11-23): a garage player carries every owned type (or only the equipped ones, CARRY setting) and switches per slot with the remappable Next front / Next rear weapon (Q / LB, E / LT). Switching never fires, never touches ammunition or cooldowns; every carried type's rounds are settled after the race. Other modes keep fixed loadouts; rivals do not cycle. Reference `docs/RACE_RULES.md`.
    - **Rival ammunition pacing** (2026-11-23): each rival follows its own spending schedule over the continuous race progress (personality curve, race length, weapon value, impulses); no absolute holds; nothing saved in the closing stage. Reference `docs/RACE_RULES.md`.
  - **Vehicle damage (M6):** condition 100 % → 0 % from weapon destructions, barrier impacts and car contacts (by severity; minor contacts are free); at 0 % the car is out (DNF, classified after the finishers).
    - One hit never retires a healthy car, and condition never changes handling.
    - Same rules for the AI. Host-authoritative over the network.
    - Reference `docs/VEHICLE_DAMAGE.md`; numbers in `src/damage/default_damage_rules.tres`.
  - **Garage races** (manual: starting money, prizes for the top five only, buy / sell after a race, selling pays less, quantity limited by money). Prices and rules are in `docs/ECONOMY.md`, the single authoritative description.
    - The career (money, rounds, equipped weapons, condition, armour) is saved in `user://garage.cfg`; NEW GARAGE starts over; a race in progress is never saved.
    - Quick, spectator, couch and network races never touch the garage and start repaired.
    - **Upgrades** (2026-11-23): Engine, Brakes, Chassis, Steering, three paid tiers each (modest explicit multipliers on the stock handling), saved with each garage, the garage player's car only; rivals and other modes stock. Reference `docs/ECONOMY.md`.
  - **Environments** (formerly Biomes / track themes; the ENVIRONMENT setup option): every environment runs on every circuit, independent of Day / Night. Handling is identical for all cars, human and AI. Internal ids and overrides stay `variant_N` (display names are thematic). Table and measurements: `docs/RACE_RULES.md`.

    | Environment | Handling |
    |---|---|
    | Green Valley | baseline |
    | Wasteland | rain, grip 0.88 |
    | Winter | snow, grip 0.74, braking 0.8 |
    | The Raceway | top speed ×1.05, grip ×1.05 |
    | Tabletop Mayhem | acceleration ×1.28 |
  - **TIME: DAY / NIGHT** is available on every circuit and environment and never changes physics. At night every car has two headlights, two taillights, brake lights and automatic high beams; Settings → DYNAMIC VEHICLE LIGHTS (saved) chooses real lights or glow sprites; Settings → HEADLIGHTS (saved, 2026-11-23) tints the player's own beams from a subtle palette (rear lights stay red).
  - **Pit lanes and split sections** (2026-11-17): every circuit has a pit lane on the main straight and one split section with a solid divider. Each pit has a through-lane and three numbered service bays, roomy enough to enter and leave the middle one with both neighbours occupied (one car per bay, whether it wants service or not; with all three held nobody else can begin a service: wait or drive on). Free pit service: +30 condition after 6 s stationary in a bay, once per traversal, no ammunition, never after a DNF, none in Knockout; Battle Royale has no pits. Pit limiter 50 %; double-tapping the accelerator binding overrides it for that traversal, free (speeding is allowed). Meaningful collisions in the pit lane (above the damage thresholds, by the car to blame, one per incident) earn a collision ticket: $100 for the human in garage races, a statistic otherwise; a banner, the user's "Pit lane violation" recording and a kill-feed entry without K / D. Rivals obey the same rules (2026-11-17, Pit Lane Refinement). Host-authoritative over the network (protocol 6; protocol 7 adds the final DNF / condition / ammunition to the results). Reference `docs/PIT_LANES.md`.
  - **Steering:** key steering ramps in over 0.03 s (standstill) to 0.14 s (top speed) and releases at once; sticks steer proportionally (deadzone 0.2).
  - **Circuits:** Kestrel Ridge (default), Larkspur Ring and Ravenhook, with elevated crossings, narrowings and speed-dependent jumps (`docs/TRACKS.md`). Legacy layouts are kept for tests only.
  - **Modes** (GO RACING!: shared setup screen with a rotating circuit preview): Quick Race, Garage Race, Championship, Knockout, Battle Royale, Spectate, Multiplayer.
    - **Finishing early** (single-player) shows a 5 s celebration, then live spectating with provisional standings; skipping fast-forwards the real race.
    - **Kill feed and TOMBSTONE | K | D** (`src/combat/combat_stats.gd`).
    - **Knockout:** ten cars, nine laps; last place still in is out at each leader lap; local only.
    - **Battle Royale:** the Scrapyard arena; no respawns; unlimited cooldown missiles / mines; shrinking zone; local single-player only.
    - Reference `docs/RACING_MODES.md`.
  - **Championship:** two rotating six-round schedules (or a three-round short version, ROUNDS 6 / 3, saved) cover all circuits, environments and day / night and end with a seven-lap GRAND FINAL.
    - Points 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1; a DNF scores 0; ties go to countback; grids reverse.
    - **Winning** claims the car of the lowest-ranked rival in the final standings whose car is not owned yet (2026-11-23; deterministic, career and standalone), in its **original colours** (`user://car_collection.cfg`). That rival races the shared junker in single-player and spectator races. With all nine cars owned, a win completes the collection.
    - **Racing for Keeps:** a won car entered in a championship is lost on a loss or forfeit and kept on a win (the stake is saved, so quitting forfeits). The own car is never at stake.
    - Reference `docs/CHAMPIONSHIP.md`.
  - **Nemesis Welcome** (2026-11-17): the game keeps a bounded, factual memory of the player's memorable moments with each rival (DNFs caused either way, finishes within 1 s, cars won / reclaimed; own single-player races only), and now and then a rival greets the player at the title with its actual car, name and a short written taunt (`user://rival_memory.cfg`; no gameplay effect). Reference `docs/NEMESIS_WELCOME.md`.
  - **Career** (2026-11-22): CAREER heads the main menu. Three stages in order: Rookie Cup (3 rounds, day, Classic), Pro Series (6 rounds, weather, night, two 7-lap races, Tactical), HyperCars Grand Championship (6 rounds, every circuit and environment, 7-lap Grand Final, Tactical); winning one unlocks the next, winning the last completes the career.
    - Existing championship rules, races, AI (no difficulty scaling), garage economy (a separate career garage, `user://career_garage.cfg`), car rewards and Racing for Keeps; title bonuses $500 / $1000 / $2000, paid once.
    - Saved atomically between rounds (`user://career.cfg`); leaving a race before finishing or closing the game mid-race forfeits that attempt; losing a stage only means trying again.
    - The ending: a skippable victory ceremony and the Hall of Fame (recorded data only); afterwards any stage can be replayed and all modes stay available; NEW CAREER (confirmed) keeps achievements, cars, the Garage Race career and lifetime titles. CREDITS on the main menu.
  - **Wheels** (2026-11-22, presentation only): wheels tucked under the body (half track 10.5 display px), front wheels steer up to 30°, the tread rolls with the distance driven (a shader scrolls the one shared wheel drawing; no extra frames), a 48×28 template wheel.
  - **Achievements & rival careers:** 30 achievements, rival careers, toasts and confirmed reset (`user://achievements.cfg`). Counted in normal single-player and spectator races; not counted in couch, network or Knockout races.
  - **Multiplayer:**
    - **Couch split-screen:** two players + eight rivals; one rival sits out in rotation; press-a-button join.
    - **LAN / online:** host-authoritative ENet (UDP 24680, IPv4) with client prediction (including jumps), version handshake and connection-quality line; no reconnect. `docs/NETWORK_PLAY.md`.

  - **Audio:**
