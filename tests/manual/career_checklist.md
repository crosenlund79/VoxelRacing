# Career — human playtest checklist (2026-11-22)

Reference: `docs/CAREER.md`. Play on Windows with a controller and with the keyboard.
Your own saves are used: note your money and cars before starting if you want to
compare.

## Start and progress

- [ ] CAREER is the first entry on the main menu; the career screen explains the three
      stages and shows locked / open / won.
- [ ] START: ROOKIE CUP opens the garage first; RACE starts round 1.
- [ ] The HUD names the stage and round ("ROOKIE CUP - ROUND 1 OF 3").
- [ ] After the race the results show the prize; Enter shows the career standings
      (CAREER STAGE 1 OF 3).
- [ ] TO THE GARAGE → buy / equip something → RACE: the race uses it.
- [ ] Winning the Rookie Cup: title bonus, a rival's car, "NEXT: THE PRO SERIES IS
      OPEN"; the career screen shows the Pro Series open.

## Saving and leaving

- [ ] Between rounds, SAVE & EXIT, quit the game, start it again: CONTINUE resumes the
      same round with the same standings, money and car.
- [ ] Leave a race through the race menu before finishing: the warning says the attempt
      is forfeited; the career screen says so; the stage can be entered again.
- [ ] Finish a race, then leave while the others are still racing: the round counts.
- [ ] Close the game window during a career race, start again: the title says the
      attempt was forfeited.

## Losing, money, Racing for Keeps

- [ ] Lose a stage: the winner is named, no bonus, TRY AGAIN on the career screen.
- [ ] Spend everything and wreck the car: the next race still starts (patched up to 50 %).
- [ ] Enter a stage with a won car (CHANGE CAR): FOR KEEPS is shown; losing or leaving
      mid-race returns the car to its rival; winning keeps it.

## The ending

- [ ] Win the Grand Championship (7-lap Grand Final at night in the snow): the final
      standings say YOU ARE THE HYPERCARS CHAMPION.
- [ ] CONTINUE: the victory ceremony (podium with the real top three cars, trophy,
      confetti, standings, music) can be skipped after a moment.
- [ ] The Hall of Fame shows the completion date and your real records.
- [ ] Restart the game: the career is still complete; any stage can be raced again;
      Quick Race and the other modes work.
- [ ] NEW CAREER asks twice, says what is kept, and starts again from the Rookie Cup
      (achievements, cars and the Garage Race money untouched).
- [ ] CREDITS reads correctly.

## Wheels (2026-11-22)

- [ ] Wheels sit mostly under the bodies; the front wheels visibly steer (up to 30°).
- [ ] The tread rolls while driving and does not strobe at top speed; it stops when
      the car stops.
- [ ] With your own `custom/graphics/cars/wheel.png`, the same applies.
