# Manual checklist — Endurance racing, track branches, pit violations, lighting and steering

Reference: `docs/PIT_LANES.md`. Play at least one 20-lap race on each circuit.

## Pit lanes
- [ ] Each circuit (Kestrel Ridge, Larkspur Ring, Ravenhook) has a pit lane on the main straight: PIT sign, divider, through-lane, three numbered bays (PIT 1-3) on an apron with stubs between them, PIT lettering, limiter lines, exit. It reads clearly in every environment and at night.
- [ ] Entering the lane: the car slows smoothly to 50 % (no jolt), and "PIT LIMITER - 50%" shows with your own accelerator binding.
- [ ] Stop in a free bay: its frame turns red, "PIT n SERVICE - REPAIRING / CONDITION: a% -> b%" with a progress bar; after about 6 s condition rises by 30 points, never above 100 %.
- [ ] Drive off before it finishes: no repair. Nudge out and back in: no second service on the same visit.
- [ ] Stopping in a bay held by another car never repairs you. With all three bays red: "PIT FULL - NO FREE PIT BAY"; wait for one to clear or drive on along the through-lane.
- [ ] Turning in and out of a bay is comfortable from the through-lane; the through-lane stays clear of cars being serviced.
- [ ] Leaving the lane: normal acceleration again, no boost.
- [ ] Holding Fire through the entry never overrides the limiter.
- [ ] Double-tap Fire in the lane (keyboard and controller): the limiter turns off ("LIMITER OFF"), with no ticket, fine, banner or feed entry.
- [ ] Ram a car in its bay, or hit a pit barrier hard: "PIT LANE VIOLATION! PIT COLLISION", the announcer line once, the feed shows "YOU [ticket] PIT COLLISION". One ticket per crash, not per scrape; the car you hit gets none.
- [ ] Gentle touches in the pits and contact on the racing road give no ticket.
- [ ] Garage race: collision fines ($100 each) appear on the results and in the garage; money never goes below $0. Other modes: the ticket only, no time penalty.
- [ ] Knockout: no service in the box. Battle Royale: no pits.
- [ ] Laps, positions and lap times are correct whichever lane you take; cutting through the pit lane gains nothing.

## Split sections
- [ ] Each circuit has one split section with a solid divider; both lanes are drivable; missiles explode on the divider.
- [ ] Rivals use both lanes; nobody gets stuck on a divider nose.

## Rivals
- [ ] Badly damaged rivals pit (more in long races), each into its own bay, and rejoin without blocking the through-lane.
- [ ] With the pit full, rivals wait briefly before the bays (Victor and Greta longest, Rocco never) or drive on; the pit lane never jams.
- [ ] Occasionally a rival (mostly Rocco) speeds in the pit lane; a ticket comes only if it crashes.

## Night lights
- [ ] Night race: every car shows two headlights and two red taillights; braking brightens the taillights.
- [ ] High beams on long clear straights, dipping behind another car and before bends.
- [ ] Settings → DYNAMIC VEHICLE LIGHTS OFF: simpler glows, still readable; the choice is remembered after a restart.
- [ ] Winter Night and Wasteland Night look atmospheric and the cars stay readable. Note the frame rate with lights on and off.

## Steering
- [ ] Short key taps on a straight make small corrections (no lurching); full lock still turns as sharply as before.
- [ ] A controller stick steers proportionally (small deflection = gentle steering).

## Network / couch
- [ ] LAN race: pit stops, bays (colours, PIT FULL), tickets and the limiter behave the same on both computers; the client's ticket appears in both feeds; host and client never hold the same bay.
- [ ] Couch: each player's pit line and ticket show in their own view.
