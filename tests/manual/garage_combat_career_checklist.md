# Manual checklist: Garage, Combat & Career Refinements (2026-11-23)

Automated tests cover the rules. These need a person (real controller, GPU, feel).

## Escape / Back in the career

- [ ] **The hub's garage:** Career → GARAGE → Escape, and pad B → back on the career
  screen.
- [ ] **The hub itself:** Back again → the title menu, with the rotating circuit behind
  it (never an empty or snowy world without a track).
- [ ] **After a round:** win or lose a round → standings → SAVE & EXIT → GARAGE → Escape
  → career screen → Back → title menu.
- [ ] **Before a race:** the garage before a career race: Escape → the grid panel;
  Escape again → the career screen; nothing forfeited.
- [ ] **Mid-shopping:** buy something, then Escape. The purchase stands, and the money
  shows the same on the career screen.

## Weapon cycling

- [ ] **Setup:** a Garage Race with homing missiles and rear missiles bought, CARRY: ALL
  OWNED.
  - In the race, Q / LB switches the front slot and E / LT the rear slot.
  - The HUD shows the active type, and the line under the slot lists the others with
    their rounds.
- [ ] **Empty types:** fire a type empty. Its count turns red and its icon dims;
  cycling skips empty types.
- [ ] **No cooldown bypass:** switch right after a shot. The new type still waits for
  the cooldown (Classic and Tactical).
- [ ] **Settlement:** the garage after the race keeps exactly the rounds left of every
  type.
- [ ] **CARRY: EQUIPPED ONLY:** only the equipped types; cycling does nothing.
- [ ] **CONTROLS:** "Next front weapon" / "Next rear weapon" can be remapped, and
  clashes are refused.
- [ ] **Old layouts:** a controls layout saved before this change still works.

## Upgrades

- [ ] **Buying:** in the garage, UPGRADES (car row) shows Engine / Brakes / Chassis /
  Steering with pips, the fitted effect, the next tier and its price. Buying takes the
  money once; tier 3 shows MAXED.
- [ ] **Feel:** Engine 3 pulls noticeably but not wildly harder; Brakes stop sooner in
  both control modes; Chassis slides less in fast corners; Steering turns in quicker.
- [ ] **Persistence:** upgrades survive a restart, are separate in the career and the
  Garage Race, and NEW CAREER starts stock.
- [ ] **Rivals and other modes:** rivals and quick races feel unchanged.

## Championship reward

- [ ] **Which car:** win a championship. The car claimed belongs to the rival lowest in
  the final standings whose car you did not own yet. The text names the rival ("YOU
  CLAIMED DANTE'S CAR!") with that car's own artwork.

## Headlights

- [ ] **Changing the colour:** Settings → HEADLIGHTS cycles with Left / Right or a
  press.
- [ ] **At night:** only your car's beams take the colour; they stay subtle and the
  road readable. Rivals keep warm white, and rear and brake lights stay red.
- [ ] **Both light modes:** check with DYNAMIC VEHICLE LIGHTS on and off, and in the
  arena at night.

## AI ammunition

- [ ] **Short races (3 and 7 laps):** spectate a few armed races.
  - Rocco and Finn fire early and often.
  - Greta spreads her rounds.
  - Nobody saves everything for the last lap, and combat continues to the flag.
