# M3.5 — manual checklist (Tactical combat, garage, achievements)

All prices, cooldowns and amounts are provisional. Note anything that feels unfair, confusing or unlike Super Cars II.

## Stage 1 — Arsenal and combat styles
- [ ] Title: **COMBAT: CLASSIC / TACTICAL** toggles and is remembered after a restart; **WEAPONS: ON / NO GUNS** still works independently.
- [ ] Grid panel shows e.g. "WEAPONS ON   TACTICAL COMBAT   3 LAPS"; **C** toggles the style, **Up / Down** still toggles weapons, **Tab** the laps.
- [ ] Classic: everything behaves exactly as in M3 (one 0.6 s cooldown shared by Up and Down).
- [ ] Tactical: after a front shot the front slot needs about 1.5 s; the rear slot can fire after a brief moment; after a rear shot the rear slot needs about 3 s.
- [ ] HUD: each slot shows F / R, the weapon icon, ammunition and a bar — gold when ready, red and filling while cooling down; "-" when nothing is mounted.
- [ ] Homing missiles (teal) curve towards the nearest car; the super missile (gold) circles your car for a few seconds; rear missiles fly backwards; each has its own launch sound.
- [ ] No Guns with Tactical selected: nobody fires.
- [ ] Spectating with Classic, Tactical and No Guns runs unattended, normal and fast (F).

## Stage 2 — Garage races
- [ ] Title → RACE shows QUICK RACE (two control modes), GARAGE RACE (two control modes) and BACK.
- [ ] GARAGE RACE opens the garage first: $300, 5 front missiles and 5 mines owned and equipped; five weapon cards with buy and sell prices, owned rounds, BUY / SELL / EQUIP; RACE! is focused (one press skips shopping).
- [ ] Buying lowers the money and plays a coin sound; BUY greys out when you cannot afford it; SELL pays half; nothing ever goes below $0.
- [ ] EQUIP mounts the weapon in its slot (front: front / homing / super missiles; rear: rear missiles / mines); the footer and the in-race HUD show it.
- [ ] Results show the prize money; top five are paid, 6th–10th get nothing. Enter = garage (Fire does nothing), Tab = next race without shopping.
- [ ] Rounds fired are gone after the race; unused rounds stay; prize money is paid once however long you stay on the results.
- [ ] Money and weapons are still there after going back to the title and starting another garage race (until you quit the game).
- [ ] A QUICK RACE uses the standard loadout whatever you bought; spectating never changes your money or weapons.
- [ ] Esc in the garage returns to the grid panel (Fire starts, Esc leaves to the title) without starting or leaving by accident.
- [ ] Tactical spectating: rivals use their own weapon mixes (homing, super, rear missiles and mines appear).

## Stage 3 — Personalities in Tactical combat (spectate with COMBAT: TACTICAL)
- [ ] Rocco (12 front missiles + 4 mines) fires constantly, sometimes at nothing, and runs dry early.
- [ ] Victor (front + rear missiles) fires rarely, after a moment's aim, and finishes with ammunition left.
- [ ] Ellie (homing + mines) shoots hastily when someone is right behind her.
- [ ] Hank (front + 9 rear missiles) punishes whoever sits behind him, more so after being destroyed.
- [ ] Maya (homing + rear missiles) picks off slow, recovering or very close cars only.
- [ ] Dante (super missiles + mines) launches the super missile with a single car nearby and sometimes misses.
- [ ] Greta (front missiles + mines) spreads her shots evenly over the race.
- [ ] Finn (super missiles + mines) is unpredictable, but never misses a near-perfect chance.
- [ ] Nina (6 homing + 2 mines) waits for high-confidence shots and fires few, but they usually hit.
- [ ] Classic spectating still looks like M3 (front missiles + mines for everyone).

## Stage 4 — Achievements and Living Legends
- [ ] Title → ACHIEVEMENTS: 30 entries with medals, "n / 30 unlocked", six show "???" until unlocked; the LIVING LEGENDS table lists each rival's races, wins, podiums and current winless run.
- [ ] Winning a race shows a small "ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED — First Flag" card at the top right with a jingle; several unlocks queue one after another.
- [ ] Spectate a few races (also at fast speed): rival careers grow by one race each per completed race, never twice; ARMCHAIR EXPERT after 10 watched races.
- [ ] Quit and restart the game: unlocked achievements and rival careers are still there; an achievement never unlocks twice.
- [ ] RESET ACHIEVEMENTS asks "Erase all achievements and rival careers?"; CANCEL keeps everything, YES, ERASE clears it.
- [ ] Races play exactly the same with achievements (nothing in racing, AI or combat depends on them).
