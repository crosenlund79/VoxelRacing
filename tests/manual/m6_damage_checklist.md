# M6 manual checklist — vehicle damage, repairs, armour

- [ ] Quick race, weapons on: a missile / mine hit wrecks the car as before and the CAR bar drops (white flash).
- [ ] A hard head-on crash into a barrier costs a few %; brushing the barrier costs nothing.
- [ ] Ramming a rival hard damages both cars; light bumping in the pack costs nothing.
- [ ] At 25 % or less the bar pulses red, "CRITICAL" shows and the car trails smoke.
- [ ] At 0 %: "OUT - DNF!" banner, the car smokes dark, others drive through it, it disappears after ~3 s; results show DNF last.
- [ ] Garage race: damage carries into the garage; REPAIR ALL / +10 % prices match $3 per 1 %; with too little money REPAIR repairs what you can pay.
- [ ] Below 30 % the garage explains the free patch-up; the next race starts at 30 %.
- [ ] Armour LIGHT then HEAVY: hits visibly cost less; the upgrade button shows the next grade and price.
- [ ] Spectating: the HUD shows the followed driver's condition; DNF rivals in the tower and results.
- [ ] Couch: each view has its own CAR bar; one player out, the other races on, then results.
- [ ] LAN / online: both computers show the same conditions and DNFs and the same final result.
- [ ] Figure-eight: falling into the jump gap costs 10 %; bridge races complete normally.
