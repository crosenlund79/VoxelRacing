# Manual checklist — Personality & Polish (pit geometry, Battle Royale music, Nemesis Welcome)

References: `docs/PIT_LANES.md`, `docs/MUSIC.md`, `docs/NEMESIS_WELCOME.md`.

## Pit bays
- [ ] On each circuit the pit reads clearly at normal zoom: through-lane, three numbered bays with room between them, no stubs in the way.
- [ ] Drive into PIT 2 while PIT 1 and PIT 3 hold cars (wait for rivals or park one yourself): in and out comfortably with normal steering, from the limiter and from a slow approach.
- [ ] Park in a bay at 100 % condition: its frame turns red and no one else is serviced there; it frees when you drive off.
- [ ] Three rivals serviced at once look natural; arriving at a full pit: PIT FULL, waiting or driving on works.
- [ ] Everything else unchanged: +30, about 6 s, once per pass, limiter / free override, collision tickets.

## Battle Royale music
- [ ] Battle Royale plays its own theme from FIGHT!; menus and normal races keep their music.
- [ ] Listen through at least one loop: no gap or jump at the restart (about 2.5 min in).
- [ ] REMATCH keeps the music going; leaving returns to the menu theme; a race afterwards plays the racing theme. Never two pieces at once.
- [ ] Music volume / mute and voice ducking behave as in races.

## Nemesis Welcome
- [ ] After some races with memorable moments (a rival's DNF of you or yours of them, a finish within 1 s, a championship car won or lost), restart the game a few times: now and then a rival greets you at the title.
- [ ] The car shown is the rival's real car (or its junker if you own its car); name, opener, reaction and taunt match what happened.
- [ ] Any key, button or click dismisses it, and the menu still reacts; otherwise it leaves after about 9 s.
- [ ] Rocco, Nina and Victor use their recordings on smug greetings; others are text only. Music dips under the clip.
- [ ] It never appears on two launches in a row, and the same moment does not come back straight away.
