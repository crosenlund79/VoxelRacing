# Manual checklist — Racing Modes, Combat Statistics & Racing for Keeps

## Finishing early
- [ ] Win a quick race: "1ST PLACE - YOU FINISHED!" / "RACE CONTINUES - WATCH YOUR RIVALS!" for about 5 s, no results yet.
- [ ] Then live spectating: the camera follows a battle; Left / Right switch cars; the tower shows who is in or out.
- [ ] Tab fast-forwards until everyone is in; the results follow (TOMBSTONE | K | D), Enter continues (Fire does nothing).
- [ ] In a championship round: the "IF IT ENDED NOW" panel while watching, "AFTER THIS ROUND" with the results.
- [ ] Retire (DNF) mid-race: the same, with "OUT - DNF!".

## Kill feed and statistics
- [ ] Top right: "ROCCO [missile] VICTOR", mines, rival-against-rival lines; a final destruction shows a tombstone.
- [ ] Results and tower: TOMBSTONE | K | D after the times; numbers match what you saw.
- [ ] Split-screen: the feed sits in the right view's top corner without covering either info panel.

## Knockout
- [ ] Last place goes out each time the leader completes a lap ("NINA OUT!", "OUT NINA" in the feed); eliminated cars vanish.
- [ ] Get eliminated yourself: "ELIMINATED AFTER LAP n!", then watch; the last car standing wins.

## Battle Royale
- [ ] The Scrapyard in each environment, day and night; FIGHT!, weapons hot after a moment.
- [ ] Missiles and mines never run out ("x INF") but cool down; no homing or super missiles.
- [ ] Hits cost condition; at 0 % the car is out for good; the zone closes after about a minute.
- [ ] Rocco charges, Victor / Nina keep their distance; matches end on their own.
- [ ] Eliminated: watch the survivors; winner banner and results; Enter: menu, Tab: fight again.

## Cars and Racing for Keeps
- [ ] A won car looks exactly like the rival's car (original colours); the rival drives the junker.
- [ ] Championship with a won car: red RACING FOR KEEPS warning; losing returns the car ("... RECLAIMS THE CAR!"); winning keeps it and adds one.
- [ ] Abandon during a championship for keeps: asked twice, then the car is gone; closing the game mid-championship forfeits it at the next start (notice on the title).
- [ ] The own car is never lost.
